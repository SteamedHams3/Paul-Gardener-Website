About PWS Tree and Hedge Services

Based in the heart of South Devon, PWS Tree and Hedge Services offer expert services, covering all aspects of tree work, hedge work and garden maintenance. Covering Kingsbridge and the surrounding areas, PWS Tree and Hedge Services provide reliable and efficient assistance to help with all your tree and gardening needs. We offer excellent attention to detail across all our services, which include:

  • Tree Care
  • Tree Felling
  • Tree Lumbering
  • Hedge Services
  • Site Clearance

Our Services

Tree Care

We are experts in all things trees and offer an excellent range of tree care services. We will undertake all kinds of tree work! These include:

  • Tree Care - Tree upkeep and maintenance.
  • Tree Felling - Cutting down of a tree.
  • Tree Reductions - Shortening branches to reduce tree size.
  • Tree Pollarding - Upper branches of a tree are removed.
  • Crown Raising - Lower branches of a tree are removed.
  • 24 Hour Emergency Services - Emergency callouts are available 24/7 for urgent tree removal blocking access or damaging property.

Tree Lumbering

We take pride in providing expert tree lumbering services, turning felled trees into quality timber ready for your building, crafting, or woodworking projects. These services include:

  • Bespoke Tree Lumbering - Slabbing felled trees into usable timber for woodworking projects such as tables and benches.
  • Wood Delivery - Delivery of high quality lumbered wood.

Hedge Services and Garden Maintenance

We are dedicated to delivering expert hedge care and garden maintenance that keeps your outdoor space looking its best all year round.

  • Hedge Trimming - Expert hedge trimming services that maintain a clean and healthy look for your hedges.
  • Site Clearance - Our expert site clearance service removes unwanted areas or debris, leaving your land clean and ready for use.
